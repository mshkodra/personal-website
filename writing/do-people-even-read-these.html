do people even read these?

July 26, 2026

Every personal website I find has a blog section.

My question is: do people even read them?

Should anyone care about the things people write about, other than the writers themselves?

Most of the people aren't interesting enough, and don't write anything interesting enough.

I'm no different from them.

Maybe one day I'll find out the answer to my question.
